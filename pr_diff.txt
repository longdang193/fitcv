[32m+## Goal[m

[32m+[m

[32m+jobs and Top-N limits. Backend identity, model, dimension, summary schema, and[m
[32m+contract fingerprint are explicit. Existing production behavior remains the[m

[32m+remain separate. No candidate receives fabricated semantic similarity.[m

[32m+[m

[32m+context, duration, and proficiency resolution. Existing `selected_support` and[m
[32m+`support_strength` values remain unchanged and continue to drive current[m
[32m+validator and generator behavior.[m

[32m+### Safe promotion evidence[m
[32m+[m
[32m+Each promoted arm has held-out recall/support evidence, latency and context-cost[m
[32m+comparison, failure-path proof, compatibility proof, and a rollback path to the[m
[32m+current deterministic behavior.[m
